The unmodified bbsc-launch.exe and bbsc.dll from that archive are bundled
only for the isolated BBport seamless-co-op experiment. LICENSE.txt,
NOTICE.txt, SUPPORTERS.txt, CREDITS.txt and mod.json are included alongside
them in runtime/bbsc. BBSC identifies its license as GPL-2.0-or-later;
third-party components retain their own notices. This launcher is not the
BBSC project and is not endorsed by its authors. No Bloodborne game files
are included.

The original traditional online mode does not load BBSC. The experimental
mode must use a separate test server and saves; two-PC seamless gameplay has
not yet been validated.
